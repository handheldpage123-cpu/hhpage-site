## The situation

Apex Pulmonary Consultants is a new pulmonology practice in Flower Mound, Texas, led by Dr. Olakunle Iluyomade. When the practice opened in early 2026 it had no website, no search presence and no Google Business Profile history. Every new patient had to come from referrals or paid ads until organic search caught up.

## What we did

Rather than launch a brochure site and "do SEO later," we launched the complete foundation on February 27, 2026: a home page built around "pulmonologist in Flower Mound," a page for each service (pulmonary function testing, sleep disorders, COPD, bronchoscopy, chest x-ray, pulmonary disease management) written for Flower Mound searches, a physician profile, patient forms, a reviews page and a blog answering common respiratory questions. We set up and optimized the Google Business Profile, connected analytics and conversion tracking, and ran a small Google Ads campaign to bridge the gap while organic rankings built.

## What happened

The site ranked for its first keywords in March and has added more every month since. By November it ranks for 47 keywords, including position 4 for "pulmonologist flower mound," position 6 for "pulmonologist grapevine," position 10 for "sleep apnea treatment flower mound tx," and position 1 for the practice and physician names. Blog posts on bronchitis vs. pneumonia and pulmonary hypertension are already drawing informational searches.

In October 2026, organic search delivered 423 of the site's 651 sessions (65%), more than direct and paid combined. The site produced 21 new-patient form submissions, 14 of them from organic search, with a 3:25 average session and a 60% engagement rate. The Google Business Profile added 115 calls and 101 direction requests in the same month.
