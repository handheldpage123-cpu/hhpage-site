## The situation

Mindful Pediatric Dentistry & Orthodontics in McKinney, Texas combines pediatric dental care and orthodontics in one practice. Before May 2026 the website ranked for 33 keywords and had nothing on page one of Google, so parents searching for a pediatric dentist or orthodontist in McKinney were finding competitors first.

## What we did

We launched a new website on May 11, 2026 with one page for every service the practice offers, each written for McKinney searches: pediatric preventive, restorative, sedation and emergency dentistry; early, adult, airway and surgical orthodontics; braces, Invisalign, palatal expanders and retainers; plus insurance, technology, team and blog pages. Each page has its own title, structured data and internal links so Google can match it to the right search. We refreshed the Google Business Profile at the same time and kept reviews flowing.

## What happened

The keyword footprint grew every month after launch: 54 in May, 97 in June, 175 in August and a peak of 193 in September. Page-one rankings went from zero to twelve, including "pediatric orthodontist mckinney tx" at position 2, "early orthodontics mckinney tx" at 3, "orthodontic treatment mckinney tx" and "adult orthodontics mckinney tx" at 5, and the core "pediatric dentist mckinney tx" at 7. Most of these are service pages ranking on their own, which is exactly what the structure was built for.

The visitors those pages bring are the right ones. In October 2026, organic visitors engaged at 72 percent against a site average of 32 percent, the site produced 26 new-patient form submissions, and the Google Business Profile logged 51 calls (up 1,175 percent year over year), 69 direction requests and four new five-star reviews.
