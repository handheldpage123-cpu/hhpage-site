## The situation

Hoffman Orthodontics serves Leawood and Prairie Village on the Kansas side of the Kansas City metro. The practice has a strong reputation and a 5.0 Google rating, but through 2025 its website was close to invisible in search: around 100 organic visits a month, eight keywords on page one, and none of the local "orthodontist" searches that bring in new patients.

## What we did

We rebuilt the site around the way patients look for an orthodontist. That meant a dedicated page for each location (Leawood and Prairie Village), a page for each treatment in each location (braces, Invisalign, early treatment, adult orthodontics), a doctor page that answers the questions parents ask, and a short blog that covers the questions people type into Google before they book. We tightened the technical side (speed, structured data, internal linking) and kept the Google Business Profile current and active.

## What happened

Organic traffic was flat around 100 visits a month through all of 2025, then climbed every month through 2026 to a peak of 731 in September. Page-one keywords went from 8 to 47. The location pages now rank first for the practice's core searches, and the service-by-location pages engage visitors at 55 to 92 percent, well above the home page. In October 2026, organic search was the largest source of traffic and produced half of all new-patient form submissions, while calls from the Google Business Profile were up 65 percent year over year.
