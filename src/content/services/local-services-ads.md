## What Local Services Ads are

Local Services Ads sit at the very top of Google for local searches, carry the Google Guaranteed badge, and charge you per lead rather than per click. For eligible practices they are often the cheapest source of new-patient calls.

## What we handle

The setup is paperwork-heavy and the ongoing work is mostly discipline: reviewing leads, disputing the ones that should not count, and adjusting budget. We do all of it and send the lead data to a report you can open any time.
