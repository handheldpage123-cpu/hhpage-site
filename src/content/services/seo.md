## SEO that is built for how patients search now

Patients search by treatment and by town, and increasingly they ask an AI assistant instead of scrolling results. Both paths lead to the same place: a clear, fast site with real content about the treatments you offer, and listings that all say the same thing. That is what we build.

## What we do each month

- Review the treatments and areas that bring the best patients and tune the plan toward them.
- Keep your Google Business Profile active, accurate and ahead of the practices next door.
- Publish or refresh one or two pages written to answer the questions patients actually ask.
- Fix anything technical that gets in Google's way.
- Report on what moved and what it produced.
