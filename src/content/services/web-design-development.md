## Why practices come to us for a website

Most practice websites fail quietly. They look fine, they cost a lot, and nobody can say what they produce. We build the opposite: a site with a job. Every page exists to get a patient to book, call or ask a question, and we measure whether it does.

## What a build looks like

1. **Strategy call.** We look at your current site, your competitors and your Google Business Profile, then agree on a page plan and a fixed price.
2. **Content first.** We draft the copy for every page before design starts, so the design serves the words rather than hiding thin content behind stock photos.
3. **Design and build.** Mobile first, fast, accessible. You review real pages, not mockups.
4. **Launch and measure.** We connect booking, forms and call tracking to your CRM, set up GA4 properly, and watch the first 30 days.

## What it costs

We keep pricing simple and lower than you expect because we do not pad the scope. The strategy call ends with a number.
