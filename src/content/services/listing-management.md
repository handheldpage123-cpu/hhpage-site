## Why listings matter more than they look

Google, Apple Maps and the AI assistants cross-check your practice against dozens of directories. When the details disagree, you rank lower and patients call the wrong number. Listing management is unglamorous and it works.

## Our approach

We find every listing, fix the wrong ones, kill the duplicates, and keep them synced. It is a fixed monthly fee and it is small.
