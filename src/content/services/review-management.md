## How it works

After a visit, the patient gets a short text or email with a direct link to leave a review. That is the whole trick. The volume comes from asking everyone, consistently, without the front desk having to remember.

With the advanced plan, every review from Google and the review tool lands in one place, you respond from there, and your best ones can be shared to social media and your Google Business Profile with a click.

Whatever you earn shows up on your website automatically, on a reviews page and in a pop-up widget, so a prospective patient sees it without leaving your site. That part is included for every practice on the Essentials plan.
