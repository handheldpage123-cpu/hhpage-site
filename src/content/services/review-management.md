## How it works

After a visit, the patient gets a short text or email with a direct link to your Google review page. That is the whole trick. The volume comes from asking everyone, consistently, without the front desk having to remember.

We watch the reviews that come in, draft responses, and flag anything that needs your attention the same day. And whatever you earn shows up on your website automatically, on a reviews page and in a pop-up widget, so a prospective patient sees it without leaving your site.
