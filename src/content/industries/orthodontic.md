## What works for orthodontists

Orthodontic patients research for weeks before booking. They compare aligner options, read about cost, and check reviews. Your site needs to be the place they get straight answers, and booking a consult needs to be effortless. Then a steady review flow and a tight Google Ads campaign for aligners in your town does the rest.
