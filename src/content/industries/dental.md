## What works for dental practices

Dental is the most competitive healthcare category in local search. The practices that win do a few things consistently: a fast site with a page for each major treatment, a Google Business Profile that is active and accurate, a steady flow of reviews, and, where the margins justify it, tightly run ads for implants or aligners.

We have run exactly that playbook for dental practices for fifteen years. It is not complicated, and it should not be expensive.
