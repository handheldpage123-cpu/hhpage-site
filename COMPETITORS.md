## What they all do

1. **Lead with outcomes, backed by named case studies.** Every serious competitor opens with a number (new patients, % growth) and backs it with a case study that names the doctor. This is the single biggest gap on our side: `/work/` is a placeholder.
2. **Hide pricing.** Nobody publishes prices. Lasso gives budget ranges; TNT gates a PDF. Our "fees explained up front, in writing" promise is a differentiator even without numbers on the page.
3. **Client count + years in the hero area.** 300+, 700+, 25 years. Ours (100+, 15 years) is credible; it needs the two follow-up stats to match the pattern.
4. **A person, not a logo.** The Tims, Dr. Fagala, the Kbizz founder. Our About page has the people; the home page does not yet.
5. **A qualification call as the CTA.** 15-minute intro or discovery call is the norm. Our strategy call fits. An embedded calendar (most of them don't have one) is an edge.
6. **AI search as a named capability.** "SEO + AI Ranking", "generative engine optimization", "SEO/AEO/GEO". We do the work; the label is less visible than theirs.
7. **Specialty landing pages as the SEO engine.** Kbizz has 32 healthcare specialties; DoctorLogic has vertical pages. Our 4 industry pages are the start of this.
8. **Dental-only as a credential.** Three of six say "this is all we do." We are broader (dental, ortho, medical, tattoo removal); the copy should make dental depth obvious rather than apologize for breadth.
9. **A free something.** Audit tool, pricing download, ebook, webinar. Low-cost lead capture for people not ready to book.

## What we do that they don't

- Transparent stance on fees and no-upsell, said plainly.
- Essentials plan: one monthly bundle that every practice actually uses. Nobody else productizes the basics this simply.
- Month-to-month on monthly services, and clients own everything. Several competitors are vague here.
- Simple, fast site; most of theirs are heavy.
- Accessibility built in and explained honestly.

## Implications for this build

Phase 1 (now):
- Home: add a short founder/team voice block (Jacob, with a line from Steven) above the FAQ. People buy from people here.
- Home and SEO page: make the AI search angle a visible label ("SEO and AI search"), not just body copy.
- Keep the stats bar; the two placeholder stats are needed before launch (see DECISIONS.md).

Before launch:
- Two or three real case studies with named doctors and numbers. This is the one thing that will make the site look like it belongs next to these.
- Google Partner badge if HHPage holds it. Google review count/rating if strong.

Phase 2:
- Specialty pages under each industry (implants, clear aligners, dermatology, urgent care, etc.) as the SEO engine.
- A low-commitment secondary CTA: "free practice visibility check" that reuses the pre-call homework we already do.
- Resources: a short guide or two (new-patient playbook) for people not ready to call.
